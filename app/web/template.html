What this is: a unified, multi-site HAB risk-monitoring network — fusing fragmented public data (FWC Karenia brevis counts + NDBC shelf-buoy winds/temperature) into one continuous daily signal per site, filling gaps between sparse lab samples. Validated on 2018 held entirely out of training (AUC 0.91).  Not yet: a long-range forecaster from clean water — that's the frontier, and it needs the integrated, ground-truthed dataset BloomCast accumulates. That dataset is the moat.
